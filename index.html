What's new · October 2026

Two minutes on VayuChat today.

Checked answers over official CPCB data, weather on a grid from our own WRF model, offline phone apps, and where VayuChat is being used, with CEEW, in Thane and New Delhi.

New · Weather on a grid

From 535 stations to every 27 km of India.

VayuChat can now answer questions over gridded weather from the Sustainability Lab's own WRF simulation, not only over monitoring stations: a value for every 0.25° cell of India, on every day of 2023 and 2024. Ask about a heatwave or a cyclone and get back a map or an animation you can download.

Ask about a heatwave

“Map the mean maximum temperature across India during the 25–31 May 2024 heatwave.”

The answer is a map of the week's mean daily maximum, up to 48.6 °C across the north, with the states and cities that were hottest. Ask for an animation instead and VayuChat plays it day by day.

Or about a cyclone

“Animate daily rainfall across Rajasthan from 13 to 21 June 2023 as Biparjoy's remnants crossed it.”

This animation is VayuChat's own GIF export, exactly as downloaded. Every map has Download image and every animation Download GIF, ready for a report or a slide.

WRF v4.7.1, domain d01 (27 km), run by the Sustainability Lab, IIT Gandhinagar. These are model simulations, not observations. Weather questions are in testing on the lab's VayuChat build and coming to the hosted app.

In the field · with CEEW

From the lab to Thane, Delhi, and the news.

VayuChat is built with the Council on Energy, Environment and Water (CEEW), which has taken it from the lab to the people who use air-quality data every day.

Thane, Maharashtra · deployed

The chatbot in Thane's air-quality decision support system

Thane Municipal Corporation's Air Quality Decision Support System, built with CEEW, uses VayuChat to answer questions in Marathi and English over data from 350+ construction-site sensors. The system is featured in CPCB's Compendium of Best Practices under the National Clean Air Programme (September 2026).

Research

Measured, published, reproducible.

VayuBench evaluates whether a model can produce an executable program and the correct answer—not merely fluent text.

Primary paper · CODS 2025

VayuBench and VayuChat: Executable Benchmarking and Deployment of LLMs for Multi-Dataset Air Quality Analytics

Vedant Acharya, Abhay Pisharodi, Ratnesh Pasi, Rishabh Mondal, and Nipun Batra

The benchmark contains 5,000 natural-language queries paired with verified programs across seven query families. The study evaluates 13 open models under a common schema-aware protocol and follows the work through to VayuChat.
